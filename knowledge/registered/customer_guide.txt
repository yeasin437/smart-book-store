Registered customer guidance: RC accounts can deposit mock funds, buy books, view purchase history, write verified-purchase reviews, and build personalized recommendations. A deposit in this demo is not a real payment transaction.
Each accepted purchase is saved in local history and decreases inventory. The purchase amount and overdraft penalty are displayed separately. If an overdraft causes suspension, ask the super-user to add enough money to make the wallet positive and explicitly reopen the account.
For customer support contact the super-user in the demonstration app. Customers cannot reopen their own suspended accounts by signing in.
