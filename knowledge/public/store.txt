Store name: Lumina Smart Bookstore. Lumina is a demonstration academic bookstore built by the student development team.
Visitors can browse books by category, author, year, and title, search cover images and internal figures, and post reviews without registering.
To buy a book, create a registered customer account. Customer registration and sign-in use a password plus a recorded voice or face sample.
Registered and VIP customers have a wallet balance. A book purchase can exceed a wallet balance, but the app adds a one-dollar overdraft fine and immediately suspends the account if its balance becomes negative. Only the super-user may deposit money and reopen a suspended account. A reopened account must have a positive balance.
Customers who make more than three purchases or spend more than $200 become VIP. VIP customers receive a 10% discount on purchases made while VIP.
Any visitor or customer can post 1-to-5-star book reviews. Ratings from verified purchasers count double. More than three eligible ratings with a weighted average below two puts a book on the no-order list. More than three eligible ratings with weighted average above four puts it on the to-order list.
The super-user maintains the list of forbidden words. These are replaced with asterisks in posted comments. Comments containing more than three forbidden words are blocked.
Book suggestions combine viewing/purchase preferences with popular books. There is also a creative personalized three-book reading journey feature.
